- Led a small research team in developing and evaluating an adaptive driving beam prototype for nighttime driving.
- Built a perception-to-control pipeline integrating YOLO11n-based vehicle detection, a temporal depth prior, adaptive six-state 3D Kalman filtering, and hardware-in-the-loop evaluation with an LED grid.
- Evaluated glare suppression, false darkening, and CPU inference latency. Control-target filtering improved the D7 M-GSSR from **26.10% to 44.49%**, while revealing the remaining real-time deployment bottleneck.
- First-authored the manuscript [*Robust Nighttime Vehicle Localization Under Headlight Saturation via YOLO Detection and Actuation-Aware 3-D Tracking*](papers/IJIT_Robust_Nighttime_Vehicle_Localization_Under_Headlight_Saturation_via_Ensemble_Detection.pdf), currently under review.
