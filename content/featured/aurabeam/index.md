An actuation-aware research prototype for adaptive driving beam control, integrating YOLO11n-based vehicle detection, a temporal depth prior, adaptive six-state 3D Kalman filtering, and an Arduino-compatible 8 × 8 LED hardware-in-the-loop setup. Control-target filtering increased D7 M-GSSR from **26.10% to 44.49%** and reduced the D6 false-darkening rate from **64.82% to 44.56%**. CPU profiling also revealed the primary latency bottleneck that must be addressed before real-time deployment. The resulting manuscript is currently under review.
